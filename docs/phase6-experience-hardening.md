# Phase 6 experience and hardening

The first-run guide now has six skippable steps:

1. ask a focused question;
2. choose source families;
3. follow the evidence;
4. inspect a claim;
5. understand uncertainty; and
6. decide the next action.

Completion is remembered with the existing local display preferences. The
guide does not block the workspace and can be skipped at any step.

The approved hardening boundaries are complete:

- light-first theme and accessibility settings remain device-local;
- mode profiles remain tab-local unless explicit save consent is attached to a
  signed-in investigation;
- live/history/enrichment/pagination/export ownership is enforced server-side;
- individual investigations and complete accounts can be deleted;
- formula-injection-safe CSV exports retain source IDs, excerpts and claim
  assessment metadata; and
- sharing remains intentionally deferred under D9. No public or profile-bearing
  share links have been introduced.
